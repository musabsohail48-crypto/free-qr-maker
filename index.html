Basic concepts of a QR code

What is a QR code?

A QR code (Quick Response code) is a two-dimensional barcode readable by smartphones, storing links, texts, and contact details.

How do I download my QR code?

Simply pick your content, customize the colors, and click the download button. It downloads directly to your phone or PC!
