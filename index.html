FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are basic QR codes free?

Yes. Static QR creation and downloads are free.

Do I need an account?

No. You only need an account to save QR codes and use account features.

Can I monetize this website?

Yes. Ad slots are included and the project is ready for legitimate ad-network integration. You must use your own approved publisher account and follow its policies.

Are dynamic QR codes supported?

The included Node backend supports redirect-based dynamic QR records and scan counts. Deploy the backend to use them in production.
